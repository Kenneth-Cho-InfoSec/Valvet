Where Valvet is distinct

Valvet puts compile-time type and borrow checks before C11 generation, then emits a native program without a Valvet runtime. That can suit small data tools where a compact language and explicit ownership model matter.

Valvet does not currently replace the depth of the Python, R, or Julia ecosystems. It has no general user-defined generic types, closures, traits, FFI, or macro system. Its Parquet and Arrow readers support documented subsets, and lazy-to-lazy joins are deferred. The 0.5.0 reference lists these boundaries.
