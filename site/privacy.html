Privacy policy

The short version: no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking cookies. This page explains the few technical details that do apply.

Last updated 31 October 2026. This policy applies to this website.

Who runs this site

Valvet is an open source project maintained by Kenneth Cho. This policy covers the website at kenneth-cho-infosec.github.io/Valvet. It does not cover other sites or services that link from this one.

The project has no account system. The best way to reach it is through the Valvet issue tracker on GitHub, where you can ask questions.

What this site collects

Valvet does not collect personal information through this website.

  • No accounts, sign-up forms, contact forms, newsletters, or comments.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or cross-site trackers.
  • No cookies set by this site.
  • Documentation search and example filters run in your browser. What you type is not sent to a server.
  • Copy buttons use your browser's clipboard. The copied text stays on your device.

Valvet does not sell, rent, or share personal information.

Local storage

The site stores one preference in localStorage under the key valvet-theme. Its value is light or dark, and it keeps your theme choice between visits. It is not sent to Valvet or to any third party.

You can remove it by clearing site data for this domain in your browser.

Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical request data such as IP address, user agent, and requested path to deliver the site and protect its infrastructure. Valvet does not receive or store those logs.

GitHub's handling of that data is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement, including how request data is processed.

Fonts

The site loads Sora and JetBrains Mono from Google Fonts. To show the fonts, your browser requests files from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Google receives technical request data such as your IP address and browser information.

Google's handling of that data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. If you block Google Fonts, the site falls back to system fonts and remains usable.

External links

The site links to GitHub for source code, issues, and the license. If you follow an external link, the destination site's privacy policy applies.

Your choices

You can clear the theme preference through your browser's site data controls. You can block Google Fonts with a content blocker or privacy setting and still use the site.

Valvet has no account data, contact submissions, or analytics profile to export or delete. For data held by GitHub, use GitHub's privacy controls.

Children

This site is a developer resource and is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

Questions or concerns about this policy can be raised in the Valvet issue tracker.
